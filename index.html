About Us

Brunouno helps small businesses, hawker stalls, home-based businesses, cafés, restaurants, retail shops, and service providers go digital — without the high costs. We build affordable websites, online ordering systems, and practical digital marketing solutions designed for real businesses, not big budgets. Our mission is simple: help you reach more customers, increase profits, and grow with confidence, all while keeping more of what you earn. No high platform commissions. No expensive agency fees. Just honest pricing, fast setup, and a team that understands what small business owners actually need. Brunouno is your affordable partner for going digital and growing online.
